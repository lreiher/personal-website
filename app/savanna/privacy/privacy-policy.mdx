# Privacy Policy - Savanna: Safari Wildlife Tracker

**Last updated: February 2026**

## Overview

Savanna is a wildlife sighting tracker for safari travellers. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

**Summary: All your data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. No account is required.**

---

## Data We Collect and How It Is Used

### Data You Create

When you log a sighting, the app stores the following **exclusively on your device**:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Photos you take or select | Attached to your sighting records |
| GPS coordinates | Tagging where you spotted wildlife |
| Sighting notes and timestamps | Your personal safari log |
| Species selections and counts | Your sighting records |

This data is stored in the app's private storage using Apple's standard iOS data storage (AsyncStorage). It is never transmitted to any external server.

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
- We do not track usage behaviour
- We do not share any data with third parties for advertising

---

## Third-Party Services

Savanna uses a small number of Apple and third-party services to provide its features:

### Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)
If you purchase the Full Version, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Wikipedia API
The Species Detail screen optionally fetches article summaries from the Wikipedia API (en.wikipedia.org). This is a read-only request that includes the species name as a query parameter. No personal data is transmitted. Wikipedia's privacy policy applies: [wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy).

### Apple Maps
The Map screen uses Apple Maps to display your sighting locations. Apple Maps may receive tile requests to render the map. No sighting data or personal information is sent to Apple as part of this. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## Permissions

The app requests the following iOS permissions:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|-----------|----------------|
| Camera | To take photos of wildlife sightings |
| Photo Library | To attach existing photos to sightings |
| Location (While Using) | To tag GPS coordinates on your sightings |

Permissions are requested only when you actively use the relevant feature. You can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

All sighting data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time:

1. Open Savanna
2. Go to Settings
3. Tap **Clear All Data**

Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

---

## Children's Privacy

Savanna does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no features designed for children and does not collect personal data from any user.
