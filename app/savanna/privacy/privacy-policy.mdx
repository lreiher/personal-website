# Privacy Policy — Savanna: Safari Wildlife Tracker

**Last updated: April 2026**

## Overview

Savanna is a wildlife sighting tracker for safari travellers. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

**Summary: Your sightings, notes, and photos stay on your device. No account is required. Some optional app features connect to Apple and Wikimedia services.**

---

## Data We Collect and How It Is Used

### Data You Create

When you log a sighting, the app stores the following **exclusively on your device**:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Photos you take or select | Attached to your sighting records |
| GPS coordinates | Tagging where you spotted wildlife |
| Sighting notes and timestamps | Your personal safari log |
| Species selections and counts | Your sighting records |

This data is stored locally on your device using the app's on-device storage. We do not upload your sighting records, notes, or photos to our own servers.

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not require an account or profile
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
- We do not track usage behaviour
- We do not share any data with third parties for advertising

---

## Third-Party Services

Savanna uses a small number of Apple and third-party services to provide its features:

### Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)
If you purchase the Full Version, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Wikipedia API
The catalogue and species detail screens can optionally fetch Wikipedia summaries, thumbnails, and article pages based on species names and the current app language. These requests are sent directly from your device to Wikimedia/Wikipedia services and may include standard network metadata such as your IP address. We do not intentionally include your sighting notes, photos, or stored sighting history in these requests. Wikimedia's privacy policy applies: [wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy).

### Apple Maps
The Map screen uses Apple Maps to render maps and can display your current device location inside the app. Apple may receive the network requests needed to load map tiles and related map functionality. We do not intentionally upload your sighting notes or photos to Apple through this feature. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Contacting Support
If you choose the in-app "Contact Developer" option, Savanna prepares an email draft in your mail app with optional diagnostic details such as app version, device model, operating system version, locale, time zone, and Full Version status. This information is only sent if you decide to send the email.

---

## Permissions

The app requests the following iOS permissions:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|-----------|----------------|
| Camera | To take photos of wildlife sightings |
| Photo Library | To attach existing photos to sightings |
| Location (While Using) | To prefill and attach GPS coordinates to your sightings |

Camera and Photo Library permissions are requested when you choose to add a photo. Location permission is requested when you open the Add Sighting screen so the app can prefill coordinates. You can revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

All sighting data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time:

1. Open Savanna
2. Go to Settings
3. Tap **Clear All Data**

Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

---

## Children's Privacy

Savanna does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no features designed for children and does not collect personal data from any user.
